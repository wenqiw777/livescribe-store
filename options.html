LiveScribe settings

Chrome launches the separately installed LiveScribe companion on demand. One-time setup, then no terminal is needed:
1. Load this folder at chrome://extensions and copy the extension ID.
2. In a terminal, once: cd livescribe && ./native-host/install.sh <EXTENSION_ID>
3. Reload the extension, come back here, and click Test.
Sent from your browser to api.anthropic.com. Get a key at console.anthropic.com.
⚠️ Consent reminder: LiveScribe asks before every meeting. You are responsible for notifying participants and following the laws and policies that apply to your meeting.
